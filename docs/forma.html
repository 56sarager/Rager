Tap to lock. Tap to leave.

One tap.
Your phone
goes away.

FORMA uses NFC to lock your phone the moment it docks. The same tap unlocks it when you're ready. What stays on your desk is a 7.3" e-ink display showing your tasks, calendar, and notes. Crafted from wood and aluminium.

Get updates

The lock

A physical
commitment,
not an app.

Screen time limits are easy to dismiss. FORMA isn't. Tap the puck with your phone and the FORMA app locks it — no notifications, no doomscrolling, no accidental unlocks. Tap again when you actually need it. The friction is the point.

  • NFC tap-to-lock via the FORMA app
  • Tap the puck again to unlock
  • Compatible with Android and iOS

The display

What your
desk looks like
without a phone.

When your phone locks, a paper-crisp e-ink screen takes over — framed in the same wood and aluminium as the puck. No glow, no alerts, nothing fighting for your attention. Just your tasks, your calendar, and your notes, arranged the way you want them.

  • 7.3" e-ink — readable in any light, zero blue light
  • Customisable layouts via the FORMA app
  • Syncs with your calendar, tasks, and notes apps
  • Powered by mains — no drain on your phone battery
