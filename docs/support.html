Frequently Asked Questions

How do I set my email address?
Open the app and tap the email field. Type in the address you want photos sent to and tap Done. The app saves it automatically — it'll be there the next time you open the app.
What is the subject number for?
The number you enter appears in the email's subject line. It's useful for keeping your inbox organized — for example, entering a job number, batch number, or any reference you want to appear on the email.
Are my photos saved to my phone?
Yes. Every photo you take in the app is automatically saved to your Camera Roll. You don't need to do anything extra.
Why does sending open the Mail app?
The app sends through iOS's built-in Mail composer using your own email account. This means your photos go through your own account — not a third-party server. We never touch your credentials or messages.
What if I don't have Mail set up on my phone?
You'll need at least one email account configured in the iOS Mail app. Go to Settings → Mail → Accounts to add one. Gmail, Outlook, iCloud, and most standard providers work.
How many photos can I send at once?
You can take as many photos as you like before sending. Keep in mind that large batches of full-resolution images may exceed your email provider's attachment limit. If that happens, try sending in two smaller batches.
Can I change the saved email address?
Yes — just tap the email field and type a new address. The app will save the updated one going forward.
Does the app collect any of my data?
No. Your email address is stored locally on your device only and never sent anywhere. We collect nothing. See the Privacy Policy for full details.
