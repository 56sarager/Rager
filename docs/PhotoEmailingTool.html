Common questions

What is C2PA and why does it matter?
C2PA (Coalition for Content Provenance and Authenticity) is the open standard backed by Adobe, Microsoft, Google, and the BBC for verifying digital media. Until now it has only been available in professional desktop tools. Signed Photo brings it to your iPhone camera automatically, every time you take a photo.
Where is the credential stored?
The credential is embedded invisibly inside the JPEG file itself. There is no external database, no blockchain, and no third-party server involved. The proof travels with the photo.
What happens if someone edits the photo?
Any modification to the image breaks the cryptographic credential. When someone verifies the photo at verify.contentauthenticity.org, they will see that the credential is invalid and the image has been altered.
How does the email sending work?
Everything sends through your own iOS Mail app. You enter your destination email address once and it persists. Set a custom subject line number for batch and ticket reference. Multiple photos captured in a session can be sent together.
Are my photos saved to my Camera Roll?
Yes. Every photo is automatically saved to your Camera Roll in addition to being sent by email.
Do I need an account?
No. There are no accounts, no cloud service, no sign-up, and no subscription. The app works entirely on your device using your iOS Mail app.

Privacy Statement

Effective date: May 19th, 2026. This statement applies to the Signed Photo Emailing Tool iOS application.

What We Collect

We collect nothing. The Signed Photo Emailing Tool does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal data, photos, or usage information. The app has no analytics, no crash reporting that leaves your device, and no backend servers of any kind.

Photos and Camera

Photos are captured using your iPhone camera and processed entirely on your device. The C2PA content credential is generated and embedded locally. Photos are saved to your Camera Roll (with your permission) and sent via your own iOS Mail app. At no point do photos pass through our servers because we have no servers.

Email Address

The destination email address you enter is stored locally on your device using standard iOS secure storage. It is never transmitted to us or any third party. It exists solely so the app can pre-fill your iOS Mail compose sheet.

Third-Party Services

The app does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or data collection services. Sending email uses your own iOS Mail app and your own email provider governed by their respective privacy policies, not ours.

Verification

Photo verification is performed at verify.contentauthenticity.org, a service operated by the Content Authenticity Initiative. When a recipient chooses to verify a photo there, that interaction is between them and that service. We have no involvement or visibility into it.

Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13. No data is collected from any user of any age.

Changes to This Statement

If this privacy statement is ever updated, the new version will be posted here with a revised effective date. Given the nature of the app - no data collection, no servers - material changes are unlikely.
