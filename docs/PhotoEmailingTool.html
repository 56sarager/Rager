What is Photo Emailing Tool?

Photo Emailing Tool is a no-fuss iOS utility for anyone who regularly needs to send photos by email. Enter your destination address once — it's saved. Pick a reference number for the subject line. Take your photos. Tap send. The app hands off a pre-addressed, pre-numbered email to your Mail app with everything attached, and steps out of the way. Simple, fast, and stays out of your way.
