Support Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective January 1, 2026

Plain English summary: Photo Emailing Tool does not collect, upload, or share any of your data. Everything stays on your device.

Overview

Photo Emailing Tool is an iOS utility that helps you take photos and batch-send them by email through your device's built-in Mail app. This policy explains exactly what data the app handles and how.

What We Don't Collect

We do not collect, transmit, store, or share any of the following:

  • Your email address or any addresses you enter
  • Photos you take or send using the app
  • Your name, location, or device identifiers
  • Usage statistics or analytics of any kind
  • Crash logs or diagnostics sent to our servers

What's Stored Locally on Your Device

The app stores the following on your device only, using standard iOS local storage:

  • The email address you enter, so it persists between sessions
  • The subject number you last used

This data never leaves your device. It is not accessible to us or any third party. Deleting the app removes it entirely.

Camera & Photo Library

The app requests camera access to take photos, and photo library access to save them to your Camera Roll. These permissions are used solely to enable the core features of the app. Photos are never uploaded anywhere by us.

Email Sending

The app uses iOS's built-in Mail composer to send emails. All sending happens through your own Mail app and your own email account. We have no access to your messages, credentials, or email account of any kind.

Third-Party Services

The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking libraries of any kind.

Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13. Since no personal data is collected at all, the app is safe for all ages.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and note the change in the App Store release notes.

Contact

Questions? Reach us through the Support page.
